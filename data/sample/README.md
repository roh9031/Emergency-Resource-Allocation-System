# data/sample/

Small scenarios committed to the repo and used in tests and demos. Each folder holds one scenario:

| File | Columns |
|------|---------|
| `sites.csv` | `id,name,demand,urgency,lat,lon` |
| `depots.csv` | `id,name,supply,lat,lon` |
| `links.csv` | `depotId,siteId,capacity`: the allowed depot → site connections (temporary, see `data/README.md`) |

Coordinates are placeholders; nothing uses them until travel times exist. Line numbers below count the header as line 1.

The expected maximum flows are test oracles. Each one follows from a cut argument: total flow can never exceed the capacity of the arcs leaving the source (total supply) or entering the sink (total demand), and the stated allocation shows the bound is reached.

## `basic/`: 3 sites, 2 depots, every depot linked to every site

Tests the normal case: several depots competing for the same sites.

- Depots: 1 (supply 12), 2 (supply 6). Sites: 1 X (demand 10), 2 Y (8), 3 Z (5).
- All 6 links exist with capacity `min(supply, demand)`.

**Expected max flow: 18.** Total supply 12 + 6 = 18 is below total demand 23, so no flow can exceed 18. It is reached by, for example, 1→X 10, 1→Y 2, 2→Y 6. Many maximum allocations exist, so tests should check the total and the constraints, not one particular split.

## `motivating/`: the proposal's motivating example

Tests that a flow method finds the allocation that a greedy choice misses.

- Depots: 1 A (supply 1), 2 B (supply 1). Sites: 1 X (demand 1, urgency 10), 2 Y (demand 1, urgency 9).
- Links (capacity 1 each): A→X, A→Y, B→X. Y is reachable only from A.

**Expected max flow: 2, with exactly one maximum allocation: A→Y, B→X.** Y can only receive from A, so A must serve Y, and then X must receive from B. A method that sends A→X first (X is more urgent) leaves Y with nothing and delivers only 1. Edmonds–Karp reaches 2 only by using the reverse residual edge X→A to undo that first choice.

## `invalid/`: deliberate errors, one per line

Tests that a loader rejects each kind of bad line and keeps the valid ones. Expected loader behaviour: skip each bad line with a warning naming the file and line; for a duplicate id, keep the **first** occurrence and reject the later one.

| File | Line | Content | Error |
|------|------|---------|-------|
| `sites.csv` | 4 | `3,Site R,7,35.65,-82.45` | missing field (5 fields, urgency missing) |
| `sites.csv` | 5 | `4,Site S,abc,6,35.55,-82.60` | non-number demand |
| `sites.csv` | 6 | `5,Site T,-4,7,35.50,-82.70` | negative demand |
| `sites.csv` | 7 | `2,Site Q duplicate,3,4,35.72,-82.41` | duplicate site id 2 (first seen on line 3) |
| `depots.csv` | 3 | `2,Depot B,-5,35.52,-82.65` | negative supply |
| `links.csv` | 5 | `1,99,5` | link to unknown site 99 |
| `links.csv` | 6 | `3,1,-2` | negative link capacity |

7 lines are rejected in total. What remains:

- Sites: 1 P (demand 10), 2 Q (6), 6 U (4). Depots: 1 A (supply 10), 3 C (7).
- Links: 1→P 10, 1→Q 6, 3→U 4, 3→Q 6.

**Expected max flow (after skipping the bad lines): 17.** Total supply 10 + 7 = 17 is below total demand 20. It is reached by 1→P 10, 3→U 4, 3→Q 3. The answer differs from `basic/` on purpose, so a test cannot pass by loading the wrong folder.

## `large/`: 200 sites, 20 depots, 400 links

Tests scale and many overlapping routes. Generated by `scripts/make_sample_large.py` with seed 42; the script's comments contain the full argument.

- The 200 sites are split into 20 disjoint groups of 10, one group per depot. Each depot's supply is 50–90% of its own group's demand, and it links to its 10 own sites plus 10 random sites from other groups. Link capacity is `min(supply, demand)`.
- Totals: supply 3990, demand 5667.

**Expected max flow: 3990 (= total supply).** No flow can exceed total supply. Each depot can ship all of its supply to its own group alone, because that group's demand is at least its supply and no other depot shares those sites. Extra links only add routes, so they cannot lower the maximum.
